# Example cover letter (style guide)

Use this for tone and flow ONLY. The agent never copies its sentences.
Replace it with a letter you like once the agent has written one you approve.
Shape: opens with their need, one idea per paragraph, linked transitions, the close follows from the proof
(CLAUDE.md section 5).

---

Dear Hiring Manager,

[Their need, in your own words, taken from the listing.] [Why this company or team appeals, using one specific detail.] [A similar problem you have faced, with one concrete detail of scale from profile.md.]

[The proof: your most relevant achievement as a short story: the problem, what you did, the result with a number from profile.md.] [A second achievement linked to it with a real connecting idea, such as "That work taught me..." or "Earlier, at [previous employer]...".]

[How you work: the two soft skills the listing asks for most, each shown through a real example from profile.md, never with adjectives.]

[The close: what you would bring to this team, and one specific thing you would like to discuss that follows from the proof.]

Warm regards,
[Your name]
